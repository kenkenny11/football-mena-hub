# Brighton pick up first away league victory over Man City

*2026-11-01 | BBC Sport | DRAFT*

Brighton pick up their first away league victory at Manchester City, winning 1-0 to condemn City to a second successive Premier League defeat.

Brighton claimed a 1-0 win at Manchester City, their first away league victory there. The result also handed City a second consecutive Premier League defeat, according to BBC Sport.

The narrow scoreline made the away milestone the headline, while City were left to absorb another league loss.
