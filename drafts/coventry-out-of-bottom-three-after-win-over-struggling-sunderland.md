# Coventry out of bottom three after win over struggling Sunderland

*2026-11-01 | BBC Sport | DRAFT*

Frank Lampard's Coventry City lift themselves out of the relegation zone after beating struggling Sunderland, who remain rooted to the bottom of the Premier League table.

Coventry City moved out of the relegation zone by beating Sunderland, BBC Sport reports. Frank Lampard’s side earned the result against a Sunderland team that remains bottom of the Premier League table.

The win changes Coventry’s position in the bottom three, while Sunderland remain at the foot of the standings.
