# Flick praises Yamal’s response as Barcelona look to reset

*2026-10-30 | ESPN | EDITED*

The Barça coach says the teenager has stayed focused after the Ballon d’Or, while defensive injuries remain a concern.

Hansi Flick says Lamine Yamal has stayed focused in training since the Ballon d’Or ceremony. Yamal placed third in the voting and won the Kopa Trophy for the third consecutive year.

Barcelona now turn to the visit of Alavés after defeats by Paris Saint-Germain and Real Madrid. Flick praised Yamal’s response and said the squad can refocus on football.

Injuries have narrowed Flick’s defensive options, and he did not rule out considering January reinforcements. Barcelona also said a supporter section will be closed for the Alavés match after disciplinary proceedings tied to the previous Clásico; the club hopes it can reopen for a later fixture if discussions resolve the issue.
