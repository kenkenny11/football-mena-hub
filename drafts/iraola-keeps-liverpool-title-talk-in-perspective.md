# Iraola says Liverpool title talk can wait until later in the season

*2026-10-30 | ESPN | EDITED*

Ahead of Arsenal’s visit, the Liverpool manager says his focus is on preparation, while two players remain possible returns.

Andoni Iraola says it is too soon to judge Liverpool’s title prospects. Speaking before Arsenal’s visit to Anfield, he said his attention is on the next match and suggested assessing the table closer to midseason.

Iraola described Arsenal as a strong opponent. Dominik Szoboszlai and Víctor Muñoz could return from injury, but he had not confirmed either would feature.

For now, the immediate test takes precedence over title predictions. The availability update remains provisional until the club makes a final decision.
