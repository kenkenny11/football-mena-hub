# Spurs revival continues with victory over 10-man Crystal Palace

*2026-11-01 | BBC Sport | DRAFT*

Jean-Philippe Mateta was shown an early red card as Crystal Palace lose 2-0 at Tottenham to increase the pressure on manager Pierre Sage.

Tottenham beat Crystal Palace 2-0 after Jean-Philippe Mateta was sent off early, BBC Sport reports. The defeat adds to the pressure on Palace manager Pierre Sage.

Palace played much of the match with ten men, and Spurs turned that advantage into a two-goal victory.
