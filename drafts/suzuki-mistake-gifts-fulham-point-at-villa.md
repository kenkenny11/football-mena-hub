# Suzuki mistake gifts Fulham point at Villa

*2026-11-01 | BBC Sport | DRAFT*

Aston Villa goalkeeper Zion Suzuki's second-half mistake sees Fulham secure a 1-1 draw at Villa Park after Nicolas Jackson had given the hosts the lead.

Fulham drew 1-1 at Aston Villa after goalkeeper Zion Suzuki’s second-half error, BBC Sport reports. Nicolas Jackson had put Villa ahead before the equaliser.

The sides shared the points at Villa Park, with the decisive moment coming from the goalkeeper’s mistake.
